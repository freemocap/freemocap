About this lab

Pause stops new dispatches; already-admitted work finishes. Step admits one work item. Cancel prevents late result publication.

Simulation only: no camera access, real recordings, model inference or video files. Artifacts are bounded to 8 MiB; at most 120 frames × 4 cameras. The scheduler is event-driven; this viewer polls every 250 ms. A resource wait is not a scientific dependency.
